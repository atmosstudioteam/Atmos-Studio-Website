01 / CONFLUX

A techno-magical world with a reason to progress.

Conflux is the current flagship Atmos Studio project: a large Minecraft 1.21.1 modpack on NeoForge, built around structured progression and interconnected systems.

01

Technology

Large production systems, complex processing, machinery, resources, automation and technological progression.

02

Magic

Rituals, spells, mysterious dungeons, ancient secrets, puzzles and progression through hidden knowledge.

03

Exploration

Varied dungeons, updated caves, hidden locations, dangerous dimensions, bosses and meaningful exploration rewards.

04

Custom Development

Custom scripts, recipes, quests, balancing, interfaces, visual design and original gameplay systems.
